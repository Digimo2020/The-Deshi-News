# TheDesi.news - Feature Documentation

## 📰 News Portal

### Overview
The News Portal is the heart of TheDesi.news, delivering curated news relevant to South Asian immigrant communities in Portugal.

### Key Capabilities
- **Curated News Feed**: Articles selected for relevance to Desi communities
- **Multi-Language Support**: Read news in Portuguese, English, Hindi, Bengali, and more
- **AI-Powered Categorization**: Automatic tagging and categorization of news
- **Search & Filtering**: Find news by category, date, language, or keywords
- **Save & Share**: Bookmark articles and share with community
- **Reading Time Indicator**: Know how long each article takes to read
- **Related Articles**: Discover similar news pieces
- **Community Comments**: Discuss articles with other community members

- Stay informed about Portugal-related news

## 🛂 Immigration & AIMA Guidance

### Overview
Comprehensive immigration support system tailored for Portugal's procedures and regulations.

### Key Capabilities
- **AIMA Guidance**: Up-to-date information from Agency for Integration, Migration and Asylum
- **Visa Categories**: Detailed explanations of all visa types (work, study, residence, etc.)
- **Work Permits**: Requirements, application process, renewal procedures
- **Residency Information**: Pathways to get permanent/temporary residence
- **Documentation Checklists**: Required documents for each visa type
- **Timeline & Costs**: Expected processing times and associated fees
- **NIF/NISS Registration**: Step-by-step guides for tax and social number
- **Healthcare Access**: Information on registering with SNS
- **Banking & Finance**: Opening accounts as a foreigner
- **Education Enrollment**: Steps for children's school registration
- **AI Assistant Integration**: Ask questions about immigration topics

### Screenshot Location
`./assets/features/immigration-guide.png`

### Use Cases
- Plan visa application strategy
- Understand residency requirements
- Prepare documentation
- Calculate expected costs and timeline
- Get clarification on complex procedures
